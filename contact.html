Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a bug?

Please use our contact form and select "Technical Support" as the subject. Include as much detail as possible about the bug, including screenshots if available. Our team typically responds to bug reports within 24-48 hours.

Do you offer refunds?

We offer refunds within 14 days of purchase if you've played less than 2 hours of the game. Please contact our support team with your purchase details. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.

How can I join your team?

We're always looking for talented developers and designers! Check our "Team" page for current openings and send us your portfolio and resume using the "Business Partnership" option in our contact form.

Where can I find game tutorials?

Visit our "Tips" section for comprehensive game guides and tutorials. We regularly update this section with new content. You can also subscribe to our newsletter for weekly tutorial highlights.

Do you have a mobile app?

Our mobile app is currently in development and will be available on both iOS and Android platforms by the end of this year. Sign up for our newsletter to be notified when it launches!
